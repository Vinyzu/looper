## Looper Player 2.7.4

### New
- Open audio files from other apps in a Dial-style Quick Play popup
- Redesigned Android search with filter chips, recent searches and select-all to playlist
- Add to Playlists bottom sheet, automatic 2x2 playlist covers and an add-songs hint
- Dashboard playlists row, ordered by when you last played them
- Set your own artist pictures; automatic ones now match the artist exactly
- More lyrics services, smoother lyrics follow, fast scroll and tuned haptics

### Improved
- Floating collection headers, scoped shuffle and better playlist controls
- Lyric lines sung together stay paired; softer word glow
- Sharper artwork, decoded for the size it is shown at
- Manually queued songs are kept when the queue changes
- Updated German translations

### Fixed
- Bluetooth next/previous buttons and resuming when earbuds go back in
- Smoother scrolling that keeps touch fling momentum
- Snackbar, Quick Picks and Open-with popup animation fixes

### Downloads
- **Android**: pick the APK for your phone (`arm64-v8a` for most phones) or the universal APK.
- **Linux**: `.deb` (Debian/Ubuntu), `.rpm` (Fedora/openSUSE), `.AppImage`, or the portable `.tar.gz` with an install script.

Verify downloads with the `SHA256SUMS` file.
