## What's New

- 1. Quick Play — Open audio files from other apps in a Dial-style popup.
- 2. New Search — Filter chips, recent searches and select-all to playlist.
- 3. Add to Playlists — New bottom sheet, automatic 2x2 playlist covers and an add-songs hint.
- 4. Playlists on Home — Dashboard row ordered by when you last played them.
- 5. Artist Pictures — Set your own; automatic ones now match the artist exactly.
- 6. More Lyrics Services — More sources, smoother lyrics follow and paired lines sung together.
- 7. Collections — Floating headers, scoped shuffle and better playlist controls.
- 8. Fast Scroll & Haptics — Fast scrolling and tuned haptics.
- 9. Sharper Artwork — Artwork decoded for the size it is shown at.
- 10. Queue — Manually queued songs are kept when the queue changes.
- 11. Bluetooth Fixes — Next/previous buttons and resuming when earbuds go back in.
- 12. Translations — Updated German translations.
- 13. Bug Fixes & Performance — Smoother scrolling, snackbar, Quick Picks and popup fixes.
